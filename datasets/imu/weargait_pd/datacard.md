# WearGait-PD (FDA/CDRH, Synapse syn52540892)

- dataset: weargait_pd
- family: imu  (physical path: datasets/imu/weargait_pd)
- MoveAtlas cells: B2, B3, C1 (age-matched controls); M2, M3
- population: 100 PD + 85 age-matched controls, 1,458 gait/balance trials
- n_subjects: 185
- signal: 13 Xsens IMUs (full 9-axis) + Moticon insoles (16 cells/foot) + walkway + video, all 100 Hz
- license: CC BY 4.0 ; redistribution: yes
- access: registration (free Synapse account + click-through)
- status (2026-08-22): downloaded + INGESTED v1: 1,458 IMU records + 1,362 per-foot force totals (insole cell->anatomy layout undocumented: 16-cell mapping deferred)
- link: https://www.synapse.org/Synapse:syn52540892
- ingestion: build_canonical_imu.py + build_canonical_force.py (totals-only)
- notes: richest inertial dataset of the corpus; also a video+IMU+force pair
- known issues (2026-09-17, 2026-09-28, 2026-09-29, 2026-10-05, 2026-11-10): the source exports gyroscope rates in rad/s and magnetometer values normalized to the local field; records built from 2026-09-17 carry the gyroscope in deg/s, keep the magnetometer as recorded (declared in mag_unit) and mark sensor dropouts in sample_valid instead of writing zeros (earlier records held 20 partly missing accelerometer traces in m/s2 under the unit g). The force records (per-foot insole totals) have no source LTotalForce or RTotalForce value while an insole is not streaming (in all 1,362 recordings, as a run at the start of 1,358 of them; 8.6 percent of the foot samples): records built from 2026-09-29 store those samples as 0 N and mark them False in sample_valid, per foot; earlier records wrote 0 N with no mask, so in them read 0 N on both feet as missing, and 0 N on one foot as possibly missing. Three subject ids keep the suffix " (control)" of the source CSV file names (weargait:NLS145 (control), weargait:NLS193 (control) and weargait:NLS207 (control) in the inertial records, the first two also in the force records), while the source demographic tables and the PKMAS file use the bare ids: strip the suffix before a lookup. The source PKMAS walkway file (Walkway-derived metrics/PKMAS Walkway Gait Metrics - HP+SP.csv) repeats the NLS166 SelfPace metrics in its NLS165 SelfPace row (identical rows; the NLS165 walkway contacts give 109.3 steps/min against 103.5 in that row). The source CONTROLS demographic table (CONTROLS - Demographic+Clinical - datasetV1.csv) gives the control HC134 (weargait:HC134) a height of 47 in (1.194 m) with a weight of 54 kg, a body mass index of 37.9: an adult height only in rare short-stature conditions, it may be 4 ft 7 in (1.397 m, body mass index 27.7) written without its separator. The table is kept as published; read height-based results with and without HC134. The source CSV files of the control WHC026 (weargait:WHC026, all 10 inertial records) carry its lateral shank and mid-thigh sensors under the labels of the other leg: in each of its 10 trials the angular rate of the sensors labelled L_LatShank and L_MidLatThigh is high while the left insole is loaded and the right one unloaded, the timing of a right-leg segment, and conversely for R_LatShank and R_MidLatThigh, while its ankle and dorsal-foot sensors follow their own foot, as every sensor does in 24 walking trials of 12 other controls; in its 8 trials with walking bouts the main rotation axis of each shank sensor also has the sign the other controls show on the other side. The records keep the source labels: for WHC026 read shank_L as shank_R and thigh_L as thigh_R, and conversely; the ankle, foot and insole channels are not affected.
