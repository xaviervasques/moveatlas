Movement gallery
================

.. raw:: html

   <div class="admonition warning">

   <p>Every clip of the gallery is generated by a body model from stated parameters. None is a recording of a person,
   and none shows a patient. The clips have not yet been reviewed by a movement-disorder neurologist: they illustrate
   how a sign moves, and are not a reference for diagnosis, for training raters or for scoring a scale.</p>

MoveAtlas gathers recorded movement data: pose, inertial sensors, EMG, force plates, silhouettes and EEG, harmonized
across datasets (:doc:`statistics`). The recordings are scarcest where the clinical need is greatest: the hyperkinetic
disorders (dystonia, tremor, chorea, myoclonus, tics, athetosis, ballismus, stereotypies) and the signs a clinician
grades at the bedside. The gallery adds the other half: a synthetic patient whose movements can be shown, graded and
compared, sign by sign.

The gallery holds |gallery_n_clips| clips in |gallery_n_groups| groups: |gallery_n_symptom| clips of a sign and
|gallery_n_level0| level 0 clips. It was exported on |gallery_created| (about |gallery_mb| MB, one page that works
offline).

What a clip shows
-----------------

Each clip is a skeleton: the 22 body joints and the 30 hand joints of a body model, drawn as bones, with a few
drawing marks fixed to the joints (the head, the ears, the heels, the big toes, the thumb and index tips) and the scene
it needs (a chair, a table, an examination couch, the floor). A clip plays beside its **level 0**: the same body doing
the same task without the sign. Where a clip carries a physiological background (breathing, postural sway, small
drifts), its level 0 carries the same one, so that the difference between the two is the sign.

The caption under each clip names:

- the examination task (seated rest, arms outstretched, finger tapping, walking, and so on);
- the scale item the clip illustrates and the grade it is built at (MDS-UPDRS part III, TETRAS, SARA, UDysRS, TWSTRS,
  and others), in the program's own words: the anchor texts of these scales are not reproduced;
- the parameters the sign is built with (a frequency, an amplitude at a stated body point, a duration), each taken
  from a cited study or marked as a value of the generator;
- a provenance chip that says, phrase by phrase, where a value comes from: a cited source, or a value, a grading or
  a reading rule of the generator's own.

How the clips are organized
---------------------------

.. raw:: html
   :file: _generated/movement_gallery_groups.html

Within a group, the clips of one sign are ordered by grade, each beside its level 0, so that a reader sees the sign
grow. Clips that share a task share their level 0.

How the movements are made
--------------------------

The synthetic patient is built in the MD_FM program, which uses MoveAtlas as its data resource. A sign is an
operator applied to a healthy movement: a tremor adds an oscillation at a stated frequency and amplitude to the joints
it involves, a dystonia holds a posture and adds its own overflow, a bradykinesia slows and shrinks a repeated
movement and inserts hesitations, a gait disorder changes the stepping itself. The healthy movements are generated by
the same program: no clip of the gallery reads a motion-capture take, nor stands beside a level 0 that does (each
clip's build is recorded at export, every file it opens). Each clip is checked when it is built: the joints within
their limits, the body continuous and in balance, the feet planted where the clip plants them, the hands clear of the
face, the head, the trunk and the thighs, the arms clear of the chair and the table; a clip that fails one of these
checks is not shown. The exported skeleton is checked again (its continuity, the contacts between body parts, foot
sliding, a body frozen still, the floor), and a clip that fails one of these checks is not shown either, with the clips
drawn beside it. |gallery_n_left_out| clips of the program's catalogue are left out of the gallery, those a check
refuses and a level 0 left without a clip of a sign beside it; the page's about section gives their number by reason.
No clip of the program's catalogue is built on a motion-capture recording.

Limits
------

- A skeleton shows joint motion only. Signs carried by the face, the voice, the eyes or the muscle tone are out of
  reach, and so is anything below the resolution of a joint.
- The grades are the parameters a clip is built with, mapped to a scale item; a grade is not a rating of the clip. A
  blind rating by movement-disorder neurologists comes before any claim that a clip reads as its grade.
- One body, one adult shape: the gallery does not show the variety of patients.
