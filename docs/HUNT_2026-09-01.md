## Confirmed hard negatives (paper findings, record in the atlas)

As of 2026-09-01, NO openly downloadable data exists for:
- **A3 tics** (every tic video/EMG corpus is institution-private; the
  Zenodo GTS EEG record is access-restricted),
- **B4 akinesia** (the space collapses into bradykinesia tapping sets),
- **B1 rigidity as measured signal** (KINARM/NeuroFlexor/Myoton
  studies all "on reasonable request"),
- **kinematic Huntington chorea** beyond gaitndd/hd_accel_jnet
  (Enroll-HD and every wearable HD study are gated),
- **pharmacological challenge kinematics** (caffeine, alcohol tremor:
  no deposits; the Dryad alcohol-dystonia TABLE above is ratings, not
  signal).
These join docs/ACCESS_BARRIERS_CASES.md as scarcity evidence.
